About Ignova Technologies

Ignova Technologies is a United States-based consulting company that helps job seekers secure employment opportunities in the U.S. market.

Many professionals in the U.S. balance full-time jobs, higher education, and personal responsibilities. Because of this, they often do not have enough time to apply for hundreds of suitable job openings every week.

Our Recruiting Team assists these clients by managing their job application process professionally and efficiently.

Our Objective

Resume Tailoring & Job Application Standards

Introduction

Resume tailoring is one of the most important responsibilities of a recruiter.

A well-tailored resume increases the chances of passing the Applicant Tracking System (ATS) and improves the likelihood of securing interviews.

The goal of resume tailoring is not to change the candidate's background or create false information.

Instead, it is to present the candidate's existing skills and experience in the most relevant way for the job being applied to.

What is Resume Tailoring?

Resume tailoring is the process of modifying a client's resume so that it aligns with the requirements of a specific job description while keeping all information truthful and consistent.

A Tailored Resume Should:

  • Highlight the most relevant skills.
  • Emphasize matching experience.
  • Include keywords from the job description where appropriate.
  • Maintain professional formatting.
  • Remain factually accurate.

Objectives of Resume Tailoring

  • Increase ATS compatibility.
  • Improve recruiter visibility.
  • Match client experience with job requirements.
  • Improve interview conversion rates.
  • Present the client's strongest qualifications.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What should I do if I don't understand an application question?

Pause the application, record the question, and contact your trainer. Never guess the answer.

Q2. Can I apply without reading the job description?

No. Every job description must be reviewed before submitting an application.

Q3. What if I accidentally upload the wrong resume?

Correct the mistake immediately if possible and notify your trainer if the application has already been submitted.

Q4. What if I cannot meet my daily target?

Inform your reporting manager as soon as possible and explain the reason.

Q5. Can I modify information in the client's resume?

Only as part of the approved resume tailoring process, and only to improve relevance while keeping all information truthful.

Q6. Can I share training materials with others?

Training materials are for authorized participants only. Share them only with permission from the organization.

Q7. Who should I contact if I encounter technical issues?

Report technical issues to your trainer or designated support contact immediately.
